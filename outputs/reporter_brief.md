Source: 2020-2024 ACS five-year estimates, B10050; B01003 for place population.

## Georgia in national context

- An estimated 89,880 +/- 3,211 Georgia grandparents age 30 or older were responsible for their own co-resident grandchildren under 18 (90% MOE).
- Among grandparents age 30 or older living with their own grandchildren under 18, 35.2% +/- 1 percentage points were responsible for them.
- Georgia's point-estimate rank: 23 among 51 states and DC. Rank differences have not been tested for statistical significance.
- Direct U.S. estimate: 31.4% +/- 0.2 percentage points. Georgia-minus-U.S. difference: 3.8 percentage points; no significance claim.
- Among Georgia's responsible grandparents, 49.3% +/- 1.8 percentage points had been responsible for five years or more.

## QA and do-not-overstate notes

- County caregiving rankings retain 73 of 159; long-term rankings retain 62.
- Place story-scale rankings retain 16 of 675.
- Each ranking has a saved audit of retained and excluded records. See the QA CSVs for specific reasons.
- These estimates count people, not grandchildren, families or households. Two grandparents may be in one household.
- Responsibility is not equivalent to legal custody, nor does it mean parents are absent.
- This is one 60-month period estimate, not a count for a single year or a time trend.
- County and place rows overlap. Do not add them together. Consolidated-government balances are not entire counties.
- Screens are editorial review rules, not Census designations of reliability. Remaining estimates still have uncertainty.
